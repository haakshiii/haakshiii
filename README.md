## `$ whoami`

Hey! I'm **Harini Akshitha K**, a Computer Science Engineering student at **SNS College of Engineering, Coimbatore** (2023–2027).

I'm interested in how systems fail, how digital evidence tells a story, and how organisations can make technology more secure, reliable and accountable. My experience and learning span **digital forensics, cybersecurity investigations, IT risk and assurance, governance and compliance, data analysis, and AI-powered workflows**.

- 🔎 Interested in digital evidence, forensic analysis and cybersecurity investigations
- 🛡️ Building my foundations in Technology Risk, IT Assurance and GRC
- 📊 Enjoy turning data into clear dashboards, reports and useful findings
- 🧠 Exploring explainable AI and risk-based decision workflows
- 🌱 Always learning; always documenting what I learn

> **My approach:** investigate carefully, document clearly, and design with security in mind.
